

Is AI a science, or is it engineering? Or neither or both? Explain.
Ans: AI is both a science and engineering. As a science, it studies how intelligence works, using ideas from fields like computer science, psychology, and neuroscience. It tries to understand how humans think and solve problems. As an engineering discipline, AI applies this knowledge to create practical technologies, like self-driving cars or virtual assistants, that can perform tasks automatically. So, AI is a mix of both science and engineering, where theory meets practical use.
